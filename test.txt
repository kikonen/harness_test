Amiga 1200, a cathedral of plastic and light,
workbench glows in the dark like a window left open
onto a summer that never quite ended.

Four megabytes dream—
enough to hold a whole world
in its copper and blitter,
enough to paint a sky
that no one asked for
but everyone remembered.

The mouse clicks on the screen
like a small, faithful heartbeat,
double-tap, double-tap,
opening doors into
a desktop that smelled of ozone
and warm CRT glass.

1992 forever.
The year the future
was still a floppy disk
you had to hold perfectly still
while it loaded,
while the little icon spun,
while the whole world
waited for you
to come back online.
